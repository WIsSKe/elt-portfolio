Journal 01
Very Young Learners & the VYL Teacher
How do very young children learn a FL? What makes a good VYL teacher? Would you like to teach VYLs?
▾
Journal 1
Honestly, I wasn't sure what to expect from a session on teaching VYLs. I've always associated young learner classes with chaos - kids not listening, forgetting everything by the next lesson. But the What's in my bag? activity shifted something in my thinking. What I found interesting was how much was happening beneath the surface of what looked like a simple task. The teacher pulling items out one by one, always repeating the previous ones - it felt almost too basic at first. But then I realized that repetition wasn't a flaw in the design, it was the whole point. For me, the most convincing moment was seeing how real physical objects changed the energy of the interaction. There's something grounding about an actual bag with an actual ball inside it that a flashcard simply can't replicate. I think children respond to that naturally. Would I want to teach VYLs? I'm somewhere in the middle. The idea of using games and movement is genuinely appealing, but I'm not fully convinced I have the patience that age group requires. If I were to approach it, I'd keep tasks short, avoid textbooks almost entirely, and rely heavily on physical materials and play-based interaction.
